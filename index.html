SEGMENT 1 — Warm-Up & Number Sense

0:00–0:10 | 10 minutes

  • •Spiral review: 3 problems from prior TEKS
  • •Number talks or Estimation 180
  • •Quick vocabulary review (2 terms)
  • •Materials: whiteboard, warm-up sheet

SEGMENT 2 — Direct Instruction / Mini-Lesson

0:10–0:30 | 20 minutes

  • •Teacher-led instruction on daily TEKS objective
  • •Anchor chart creation with students
  • •Think-aloud with worked examples (I Do / We Do)
  • •Guided notes or graphic organizer
  • •Check for understanding: thumbs up / cold call

SEGMENT 3 — Small Group Rotations

0:30–1:20 | 50 minutes (3 × ~16 min + transitions)

🧑‍🏫 Group A: Teacher Table

Targeted small group instruction, scaffolded practice

📝 Group B: Independent Practice

TEKS-aligned problems, work mat, self-check

💻 Group C: Tech / Enrichment

Digital tools, extensions, math games

Transition signal: timer bell/chime (2 min between rotations)

SEGMENT 4 — Exit Ticket & Closure

1:20–1:30 | 10 minutes

  • •1–2 STAAR-style questions on today's TEKS objective
  • •Students self-assess (green / yellow / red)
  • •Teacher quick-sort cards into 3 piles for next-day grouping
  • •Preview tomorrow's objective

📊 Flexible Adjustment Guide

Assessment Day

Extend exit ticket to 20 min, reduce rotations to 40 min

New Concept Introduction

Extend DI to 30 min, reduce rotations to 40 min

Review Day

Skip DI, run 4 shorter rotations (12 min each)

Sub Day

Pre-made independent work packets, no rotations needed

Section B: Grouping Strategies

1. Data-Driven Groups

Based on exit ticket data — sort daily. Pink = needs reteach, Blue = approaching, Green = on/above level. Teacher table targets Pink group first.

2. Flexible Groups

Groups change weekly based on weekly quiz data. Use a class tracker sheet to record group assignments each week.

3. Interest/Choice Groups

For enrichment weeks, students self-select tasks from a choice board. Great for boosting engagement and ownership.
